# Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Approved copy, product names, URLs and legal fields are documented before release.
- [ ] Production assets are supplied separately from the screenshot and recorded in `ASSET_MANIFEST.csv`.
- [ ] MJML source remains modular under `src/sections/`; compiled HTML is generated under `dist/`.
- [ ] All essential headings, descriptions, product names, CTAs and legal text remain live HTML.
- [ ] Browser checks are run at 320px, 390px, approximately 600px and 1440px.
- [ ] No horizontal overflow occurs at mobile widths.
- [ ] Images have meaningful alt text or intentional empty alt values.
- [ ] Gmail, Apple Mail and relevant Outlook versions are explicitly tested or marked untested.
- [ ] Dark mode, image blocking, CTA destinations and unsubscribe behavior are checked for the chosen ESP.
- [ ] Plain-text fallback and ESP multipart delivery are confirmed.
- [ ] Final image URLs are HTTPS or valid ESP asset references.
- [ ] Campaign owner approves the final content and release.
